# Mama Tolu's Kitchen: business details

Fictional business for the TSG Labs build exercise, modelled on Lagos WhatsApp food vendors.

## Who we are
- Owner: Mrs Tolulope Adeyemi ("Mama Tolu"), plus four staff.
- Address and pickup point: Herbert Macaulay Way, Yaba, Lagos.
- Home-style Nigerian food: jollof, fried rice, amala, pounded yam, ofada, small chops and drinks.

## Hours
| Day | Open | Last orders |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Saturday | 10:00 am to 8:00 pm | 7:30 pm |
| Sunday | 1:00 pm to 6:00 pm | 5:30 pm |

Outside these hours, take no order and send no link. Tell the customer when we open next and invite them to message then.

## Delivery and pickup
Fees and windows come from `get_menu`; this table is for answering questions only.

| Zone | Areas | Fee | Window after payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| yaba | Yaba, Akoka, Sabo | ₦1,000 | 30 to 50 min |
| surulere | Surulere, Ebute Metta | ₦1,500 | 45 to 70 min |
| ikeja | Ikeja, Maryland, Ojota | ₦2,500 | 60 to 90 min |
| lekki | Lekki, VI, Ikoyi | ₦3,500 | 75 to 120 min |
| pickup | At the kitchen | ₦0 | Ready in 20 to 30 min |

- The window starts when payment is confirmed (PAID), not when the order is placed.
- Lagos traffic and rain can cause delays. If a customer asks where their food is after the window has passed, apologise and say a staff member will check with the rider. Do not invent a rider location.
- Other areas are not covered yet. Offer pickup.

## Payment
- Every order is prepaid through the Paystack link: card, bank transfer or USSD on one page.
- No cash on delivery, and no direct transfers to an account number.
- The link and reference belong to one order. A new order gets a new link.
- A declined payment can be retried on the same link.

## Refunds, complaints and special requests
- Refunds, missing items, wrong orders and complaints always go to the owner. Say: "I'm sorry about that. I've passed this to Mama Tolu and she'll get back to you today." Never promise a refund, discount or free item.
- Orders of more than 20 of one item, event or party orders, and corporate orders also go to the owner, who calls the customer.

## Allergies and dietary questions
- Dishes may contain peanuts (in some stews), crayfish, shellfish, egg (moi moi) and pepper.
- If a customer mentions an allergy, say you'll check with the kitchen and that the owner will confirm before they pay. Do not promise that a dish is allergen-free.
- Spice level can't be changed per order.
